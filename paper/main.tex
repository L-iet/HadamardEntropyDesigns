\title{Pairwise-independent designs: sharp entropy, concentration,\\and obstructions to global repair}
\author{Joshua Mark\thanks{ORCID: \href{https://orcid.org/0009-0003-5905-2382}{0009-0003-5905-2382}. AI tools assisted with research, mathematical derivation, computation, exposition, consolidation, checking and Lean formalization.}}

\begin{abstract}
We construct exactly unbiased pairwise-independent sign distributions whose
entropy approaches the minimum while their total variation distance from
every Hadamard mixture approaches one. We prove sharp dimension-sensitive
collision and Shannon entropy bounds, characterize equality by Hadamard
completion, and prove attainment equivalent to Hadamard existence at the
associated order. For every fixed bound on the number of observations
replaced in one move, infinitely many known Hadamard orders yield distinct
designs that cannot be connected while preserving first and second moments.
Further results give concentration, exact rounding, support rigidity and
quantitative repair barriers. The formalization and selected submission
scope are distinguished below.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Previously established inputs and formal scope}
The original asymptotic-existence project proves the unconditional multiplier
bound $\tau(u)=O(\log u/\log\log u)$ for positive odd $u$ and its uniform
Hadamard-gap consequence. These are cited inputs, not new claims here.
The spanning-slope theorem referred to later is proved in the design sections
below: with $h=2^s$, known Hadamard order $k$, and $s\le h-k<h/2$, the
finite-field construction gives an isotropic uniform $h^2$-atom law on $kh-1$
bits, a strict local collision maximum, with TV distance $1-1/k$ from every
Hadamard mixture.
The Lean development proves the sharp residue-sensitive collision bound
and both entropy inequalities for actual pairwise-independent Boolean-cube
laws, uniformity and exact support size at equality in every residue class,
and actual Hadamard completion retaining the original atom coordinates.
The converse constructs an attaining Boolean-cube law from a Hadamard
matrix, proving the exact attainment equivalence. The nonnegative defect
decomposition, quantitative weight stability, exceptional-pair bound and
explicit small-deficit extraction theorem are also formalized. The positive
degree-two Fourier correction, exact collision and variation bounds, and
its sufficient input window for Hadamard extraction are formalized as well.
The quantized obstruction for non-isotropic uniform candidate supports,
minimal-support reweighting rigidity, and the exact minimum and sharp loss
bounds along a specified straight probability bridge are also formalized.
The full-row-rank correction theorem is formalized with its actual Gram
inverse, positivity margin, and all three quantitative error bounds.
Its stated small-error criterion is connected to exact Hadamard extraction.
The finite support-reduction algorithm is formalized with its exact
face-dimension step bound, minimum-ratio endpoint rule, collision-monotone
interpolation, and terminal vertex. Its ordered-field proof includes an
explicit rational specialization.
The rational path terminates at a vertex of the real moment polytope;
the real-rank step bound is proved without identifying rational and real
extremality by assumption.
The additive-cover rigidity theorem and its missing-direction probability
laws are formalized, including the affine-hyperplane endpoint supports.
The smallest-seed obstruction and its explicit collision gap, and the
approximate-moment Parseval bound, are formalized as well.
Every maximum orthogonal subset of the augmented character trap has a
formalized Hadamard completion retaining all selected rows, with exactly
the maximum possible number of trap rows. The actual observed trap law
is also formalized: its $h^2$ seed pairs yield distinct atoms, uniform
weights, total mass one, zero means and pairwise correlations, and
collision $1/h^2$. The compressed endpoint is also proved feasible,
uniform on $2h$ atoms, and globally collision-maximizing. It has exactly
$h$ trap atoms in common. Every global maximizer has at most this
overlap and a pointwise no-better straight bridge. The constructed
bridge's exact polynomial, minimum at $1/h$, and collision loss
$1/(2h^3)$ are formalized. For finite binary linear observations, the
actual uniform pushforward law is proved uniform on $2^r$ image atoms
with collision $2^{-r}$. The consequences of preserving rank or
dropping it by one are also checked. The actual rewiring masks, initial
swap, one-column replacement rule, and nonrepeating schedule of $h-1$
jumps are formalized. Every endpoint law is feasible, and the final law
equals the compressed endpoint above. The actual Boolean laws are
identified with their binary linear encoders. Every replacement is a
rank-one linear repair, endpoint collision is nondecreasing, and the
formal counts give $s-1$ support-halving, collision-doubling jumps and
$h-s$ support-preserving jumps. The formal joined affine segments start at
the trap law, end at the compressed law, remain feasible, and have exact
attained minimum collision $3/(4h^2)$.
The coding, instability and rounding results are individually tracked in
ALIGNMENT.json. Existing source proofs cover 84 of the 89 principal claim
records. The default paper-level comparison selects the three headline
result families, rather than every formalized record; the new packaging
awaits mechanical verification.
