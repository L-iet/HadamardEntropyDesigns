\section{A summary input for the quantitative stability transfer}
\begin{theorem}[Spanning-slope input]
Let $h=2^s$, let $k$ be a known Hadamard order, and assume
$s\le h-k<h/2$. There is an exactly unbiased pairwise-independent
uniform law on $h^2$ atoms and $kh-1$ bits. It is a strict local
collision maximum in the full moment polytope, and has collision
$1/h^2$ and total variation distance exactly $1-1/k$ from the convex
hull of all order-$kh$ Hadamard laws. Its additive R\'enyi entropy
excess is $\log(h/k)$ for every entropy order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The spanning-slope construction and sharp distance are proved in
Theorem~\ref{s25:thm:design-spanning-slopes}. The compressed law has
$h^2$ equal-weight atoms, so every positive-order R\'enyi entropy,
including the Shannon limit, equals $\log(h^2)$. Subtracting
$\log(kh)$ gives $\log(h/k)$ as stated. The same theorem proves
existence of the required omitted slope set under $s\le h-k<h/2$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Formalization.}
The independent \texttt{SequenceChallenge} verifies this summary input. The construction derives its auxiliary
arithmetic hypotheses, produces an actual complete finite witness,
and proves that the same Hadamard optimizer attains the entropy
minimum for every positive order and for min-entropy. The common
excess is exactly $\log(h/k)$.
