NEXT RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. What extra structure permits progress beyond a half-frame?
The conditional arithmetic theorem gives n/2-n^(1/2+o(1)) rows,
but the structured order20 example cannot complete without changing
prescribed rows. A useful theorem must allow row replacement or avoid
such frames; monotone completion alone is false.

2. Can residual dilation variables be used constructively?
The parameter V V^T=nI_(b-a) gives all completions with three prescribed
blocks. Rational V always exists, but an exact energy gap can prevent
sign rounding. Seek an operation changing prescribed rows while
controlling the residual sign equations, not an unsupported flatness claim.

3. Find a genuinely uniform completion-count certificate.
Cubic moments improve finite lower bounds but still fail on the weaker
retained space. Higher ratios converge to the finite maximum without a
uniform bound on the useful moment order or computation cost.

4. Sharpen dimension-dependent stability in the correct model.
The existing multiplier theorem now yields exact full-bit laws with
absolute deficit n^(-3/2+o(1)) yet distance tending to one from every
Hadamard mixture. This constrains full-bit stability guarantees, not
the earlier N-4-bit extraction theorem. Keep the hypotheses separate.

5. Audit conditional assumptions and priority.
No announced GRH proof was validated here. The partial-matrix result
remains conditional on the precise Dirichlet hypotheses. Compare the
new quantitative corollaries with current literature before novelty claims.
