Round10 general feasible exchanges

bridges.tex proves a finite exact kernel endpoint algorithm taking any
nonvertex pairwise-independent law to a vertex with strictly increasing
collision, while preserving all moments and positivity. It is an
elementary pipage-type endpoint principle, not claimed novel. It may
stop at a suboptimal vertex.

The exact straight-bridge barrier to a supplied better target is given
and sharply bounded. An explicit five-bit four-coset Walsh construction
shows a selected-target barrier can coexist with a zero-barrier route
to another optimum. No universal higher-dimensional escape claim or
unknown Hadamard target is supplied.

coset laws, bridge formulas, barriers, and the full finite nullspace
endpoint algorithm from the uniform five-bit cube. No external code,
