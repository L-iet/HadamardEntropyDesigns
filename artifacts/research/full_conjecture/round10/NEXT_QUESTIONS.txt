NEXT TARGETS

1. Prove large restricted-alphabet counts, rather than just compatibility.
   Self-duality alone can give zero sign words. Even five prescribed
   orthogonal rows can leave a self-dual extension with only eight
   sign classes at order20. A general theorem must exploit additional
   structure or permit changes of code.

2. Understand code-neighbor exchanges that replace old rows.
   Extension-guided pivots preserve all old sign words, but a terminal
   real partial frame makes such growth impossible. A viable all-order
   argument must avoid those frames or exchange some of their rows.
   Do not assume every incomplete orthogonal frame has an extension.
   The new incidence criterion is M_G-r_C >
   (r_C-b_G)*p^(h-1), with h the retained-space codimension in C.
   Find conditions forcing this surplus for some retained space G;
   unrestricted averaging can be negative and is insufficient.
   At h=1 the modular pool is exactly the union of two codes, so
   no further clique search is needed, but the count remains essential.

3. Connect concentration and coding without assuming a target exists.
   Nullspace elimination reaches a vertex, possibly a suboptimal one.
   Bridges to an already-known optimum are conditional tools.
   A proof needs an actual unknown-order target or an escape guarantee.

4. Expand approximate constructions beyond the excluded seed models.
   The new error floor assumes exact balance, uniform seed weights,
   and output degree<=r-2. Nonuniform laws, different seed sizes, or
   higher-degree constructions are not covered by that obstruction.

None of these tasks is resolved merely by an equivalent reformulation
of the conjecture or successful computations at known orders.
