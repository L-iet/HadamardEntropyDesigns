ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE

Fourth-cycle phase invariants and complex Hadamard equivalence:
Tadej and Zyczkowski, A concise guide to complex Hadamard matrices:
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0512154
The finite dephasing average and rounding thresholds are independently
derived here. Priority for these elementary estimates is unestablished.

Conference normalization and orthogonal-design background:
Geramita and Verner, Orthogonal Designs with Zero Diagonal (1976):
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1976-028-3
The conference normalization theorem is an explicit input where used;
it is not claimed as a new result.

Hadamard energy minimization and local traps are established topics:
Finding a Hadamard Matrix by Simulated Quantum Annealing (2018):
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7512635/
The general phenomenon of local trapping is not claimed new.
The branch records exact finite witnesses and tensor formulas.

The Bloch-sphere representation of two-dimensional quantum pure
states is classical. A residual two-dimensional projection is used
as a completion criterion, not as an all-order existence theorem.
Additional primary sources appear in the branch proof.
The real codimension-four completion input is due to Marshall Hall.
Verheiden's primary dissertation describes this input and its
codimension-seven extension:
https://thesis.caltech.edu/2899/
The automatic quaternary two-column completion consequence must not
be advertised as an independent replacement for that classical input.

This pass does not establish bibliographic novelty or mathematical
priority. Public mathematical papers were consulted; no implementation
from them was copied. All certificate arithmetic is independently coded.
