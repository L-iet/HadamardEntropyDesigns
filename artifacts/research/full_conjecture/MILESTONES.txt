RESEARCH MILESTONE: TENSOR STABILITY
Recorded 20 November 2026. Responsible human author: Joshua Mark.
ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0003-5905-2382

For every finite real p>2, every nonflat critical point U of
F_p(U)=sum_ij |u_ij|^p on O(n), n>=2, has a negative tangent-Hessian
direction at some finite self-tensor power U^(tensor k).
Consequently U is a scaled real Hadamard matrix if and only if every
self-tensor power of U is a local minimum of the corresponding energy.
The proof also applies to real matrices with orthonormal rows.

Preserved proof: round3/geometry/tensor_stability.tex
Readable paper: round3/geometry/tensor_stability.pdf (5 pages)
Exact finite checks: round3/geometry/check.py and checks.json (39 cases)
Integrity and scope record: round3/verification.json
Broader third research report: round3/research.pdf (13 pages)

The mathematical proof establishes the uniform assertion; the finite
checks are supporting checks, not a replacement for that proof.
Priority and novelty have not been established by a comprehensive
literature review. This theorem does not prove existence of a Hadamard
matrix in any additional dimension. A lifted descent direction lives
in a larger orthogonal group; no descent to the original dimension
has been proved. Lean formalization is deferred. No Git commit made.

Full report: round15/research.tex/pdf (8 pages).
Focused theorem: round15/phase_escape.tex/pdf (2 pages).

Every real non-Hadamard sign matrix is a strict saddle of squared
Gram error on the complex phase torus. Constant-energy pairwise
unitary row/column mixing supplies zero-curvature tangents. Their
Hessian images either construct a negative direction, or force a
scaled partial isometry whose two kernels supply one. An exact
integer direction and Gaussian-rational Cayley backtracking give
a finite strictly decreasing phase step. This escapes all real
discrete traps but does not guarantee convergence or quantization.
All 512 normalized order-four matrices and larger/singular cases
were checked exactly; the general result has a paper proof.

Using classical real completion with at most seven missing rows,
even-order quaternary frames missing at most three columns complete.
A systematic Gaussian-rational kernel algorithm checks at most 21
candidate columns in total. Defect four reduces to a 64-test first
column gate; automatic success is not asserted. Classical input is
explicitly credited, and the large starting partial frame is missing.

Hadamard row layers have energy m^2 times the sum of base energies.
The explicit tensor trap has a three-strip escape with exact strip
barrier 8m^2 and a four-block-row monotone repair to Hadamard; this
uses 4m ordinary row updates and changes 7m^2 entries, not optimally
claimed. It is a repair of that family, not a general convergence rule.

A normalized H4/2 four-row switch preserves the weighing equation

motion avoids a proved local rigidity obstruction. No universal
