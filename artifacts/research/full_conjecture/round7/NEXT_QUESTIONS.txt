NEXT CONSTRUCTION TARGETS

1. Larger or nonlinear seeds for non-power-of-two orders.
Fixed linear observables on the smallest binary seed force uniformity.
A larger seed can have missing Fourier directions, but single-character
compression only gives power-of-two supports. Investigate several free
Fourier directions jointly, with nonnegative weights and provable
collision concentration. Do not assume their feasibility at the target.

2. Concentrated weighted approximate inputs.
The correction theorem can use a law with collision>=1/N and Fourier
moment maximum B<=1/(2048N^3). UniformN-point nonisotropic candidates
cannot meet the goal through this affine repair, even with its optimal
buffer for N>=20. A new construction must supply more than integer
near-orthogonality of equal-weight rows, or use a different repair.

3. Generalize the actual trap escape.
The explicit two-block character construction admits optimal support
overlap and a finite nonlocal rewiring route. Determine what structure
in a broader local optimum would permit an analogous change of
observables or a controlled-loss jump. No universal escape theorem is
proved, and optimality of the bridge is only among straight mixtures.

4. Odd-coset shortness rather than parity feasibility alone.
The lattice alternative exactly decides absence of an all-odd integer
kernel vector. The norm40 example proves that this does not decide
whether a sign row exists. Any lattice-based existence argument needs
a justified length bound or a repair selecting a different row space.
Sparse parity witnesses can already guide which rows must be deleted.

5. Prior-art review remains necessary.
Character identities, code rewiring ingredients, arithmetic lattices
and Fourier projection are classical. Compare the exact landscape,
buffer-loss and repair statements with the literature before claiming
priority. Preserve the distinction between a sufficient extraction
condition and a theorem constructing its input at every order.
