NEXT MATHEMATICAL QUESTIONS

1. Uniform activation versus arbitrarily delayed activation.
The five-dimensional reflection proves that five copies can be
necessary as p approaches 2 from above. It does not show that five
always suffice, or that activation indices are unbounded. A useful
target is a uniform bound at fixed p or a rigorously certified family
violating every such bound. Small moment deficit alone does not
prove delayed activation: the full trace is sufficient, not necessary.

2. Tensor-compatible replacement for ordinary compactness.
An original-dimensional global minimum is not necessarily stable
under self-tensoring. One must prove existence of a stable point or
find an entirely different implication. Merely optimizing a tensor
power and invoking compactness in the base group does not provide it.
The order-6/order-36 obstruction forbids an unrestricted extraction
principle, so any such theorem needs essential admissibility or
additional structural hypotheses.

3. Non-product extraction that preserves a quantified gain.
Independent-factor projection removes the quadratic gain of our
trace-free interaction witness. Partial-trace/polar extraction can
erase the improvement identically. A new extraction mechanism must
retain information about interactions rather than just one-factor
tangents or relative partial traces. No such mechanism is proved here.

4. Complex one-copy local maximality at p=1.
The trace theorem excludes nonflat maxima that persist at EVERY
self-tensor power. It does not exclude a nonflat one-copy maximum.
Proving tensor closure for these local maxima would bridge that gap,
but closure of balanced critical points is not closure of maxima.
Neither closure assertion nor the full one-copy conjecture is proved.

5. Bibliographic review before any priority claim.
Check citation descendants of Banica--Nechita 2013 and 2017, their
general norm-optimization formulas, tensor stability in matrix
optimization, and compact-group Laplacian methods. The present
targeted search is insufficient to establish novelty. Reading papers
does not authorize copying unlicensed source code; no such code was
used in this pass.
