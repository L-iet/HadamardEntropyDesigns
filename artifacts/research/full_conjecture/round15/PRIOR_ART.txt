PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL INPUTS AND LIMITS

Motta and Shipman, Informing the structure of complex Hadamard matrix
spaces using a flow, DCDS-S12(2019),2349--2364:
https://doi.org/10.3934/dcdss.2019147
Background for complex phase flows and local Hessian analysis.
The real-sign strict-saddle argument and its integer certificates are
derived independently here; priority has not been established.

Verheiden, Arithmetical Properties of Combinatorial Matrices (1978):
https://thesis.caltech.edu/2899/
Classical real completion with at most seven missing rows is an input
to the quaternary defect-three theorem. This theorem is not claimed
as a proof from scratch or a new replacement for classical completion.

Earlier branch proofs record conference normalization, orthogonal designs,
tensor products and energy-search background. All new computational
certificates are original; earlier local certificates may be read as data.
No external code or proof-source implementation is imported.

Finite checks support the written general proofs. They do not prove
all-order attainment, convergence of continuous phase optimization,
novelty, or the full Hadamard conjecture.
