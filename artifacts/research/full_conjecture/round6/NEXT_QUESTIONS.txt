NEXT EXISTENCE TARGETS

1. Construct, rather than assume, the near-extremal input.
For N divisible4, the round6 rounding theorem only needs N-4 bits,
exactly unbiased and pairwise independent, with collision deficit
from1/N at most1/(1024N^4). This is a sufficient target. No general
construction in unknown orders is proved. Arbitrary deletion or
renormalization can destroy exact moments.

2. Escape certified collision traps globally.
The full moment polytope has explicit nonglobal strict local maxima,
even when the target Hadamard matrix exists. Ordinary continuous
monotone collision ascent cannot leave them. A successful global
principle must justify nonlocal jumps or controlled temporary loss.
No such convergence theorem follows from convexity of the objective.

3. Use limited symmetry, not full coordinate symmetrization.
The full permutation-invariant class has maximal collisionTheta(d^-2)
and Shannon entropy nearly d*log2, far from the desired log(d) scale.
This does not exclude smaller group actions, algebraic designs or
carefully chosen orbit unions with provable entropy concentration.

4. Generalize constructive repair using its exact budget.
The repeated-seed family has L<=k*s^2 and explicit efficient repairs.
The rule finds structured fibers in that family, but no theorem finds
such structure in general partial Hadamard matrices. The residual
integral factorization alone is insufficient to provide signs.

5. Preserve proof and provenance discipline.
The entropy characterization and all subsequent conditional theorems
are not existence proofs. Compare priority against weighted orthogonal
arrays, convex quadratic maximization and classical Scarpis machinery.
All external mathematical inputs are cited; no unlicensed proof code
is included. The full Hadamard conjecture is still the goal and remains
unresolved by this work.
