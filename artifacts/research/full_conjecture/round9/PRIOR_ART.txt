The 2018 Mathematics Stack Exchange question by Sasha Kozachinskiy,
"Minimal possible entropy of 4 pairwise independent unbiased Bernoulli
random variables", explicitly asks for the same four-bit Shannon
minimum and cites the Gavinsky--Pudlak log(d+1) bound:
https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/2958203/

The displayed answer proposes vertex enumeration of the five-dimensional
moment polytope; it does not list the26vertices or give the minimum.
Our analytic hull, entropy-order diagram and exact circuit/barrier
proofs were derived in this project. That independent derivation and
the unfinished public discussion do not establish priority: published
entropy optimization, weighted orthogonal-array and polytope literature
still need a deeper comparison.
