NEXT RESEARCH TARGETS

1. Construct rather than correct the concentrated distribution.
The extraction theorem needs N-4 pairwise-independent unbiased bits
with collision within 1/(1024 N^4) of 1/N. The rank obstruction shows
that small moment errors and closeness to some feasible law do not
provide a support-preserving route to this threshold. Seek a global
support replacement principle with quantitative concentration control.

2. Move beyond low-degree minimal-seed models.
The support criterion concerns encodings of existing Hadamard matrices;
it is not an existence theorem. Study nonlinear or larger-seed models
that generate orthogonality without assuming the desired matrix.
Separate representational expressiveness from constructive existence.

3. Generalize the exact exchange landscape.
The four-bit barrier is globally optimal, including curved paths.
Find a larger-dimensional escape theorem or a counterexample to a
proposed uniform escape bound. The small polytope alone supplies neither.

4. Turn modular counts into positive extension counts.
The lifting identity handles N=4(q+1), q odd, under a row-count
condition, hence only an eight-divisible class. Seek lower bounds on
M_q exceeding the exceptional-pair correction, and an independent
argument for orders congruent to four modulo eight. Partial frames
can be terminal; unrestricted extension of every frame is false.

These are open tasks, not deductions established in this pass.
