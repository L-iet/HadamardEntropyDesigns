PRIMARY SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION

Butson, Generalized Hadamard matrices (1962):
https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1962-0142557-0
Four-phase Hadamard matrices and their realification are classical.

Egan and collaborators, generalized Hadamard morphisms:
https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/73/ajc_v73_p501.pdf
Related classical phase-alphabet morphisms.

Tadej and Zyczkowski, A concise guide to complex Hadamard matrices:
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0512154
Equivalence invariants, Fourier matrices and block deformations.

Geramita and Verner, Orthogonal Designs with Zero Diagonal (1976):
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1976-028-3
Primary reference for the classical conference normalization results.

Orrick, Switching operations for Hadamard matrices:
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507515
Background on sign-matrix switching; not a claimed source of the
specific repair-energy theorem.

Kotowski and Oszmaniec, Pretty-good simulation of all quantum measurements:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09339
Relevant unrestricted measurement simulation and Naimark results.
Their near-flat effects concern weights, not entrywise-flat sign vectors.
The fixed-sign-label TV obstruction here does not contradict that work.
